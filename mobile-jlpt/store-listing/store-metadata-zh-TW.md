## App Review Notes（Apple）
JLPT Study Lab does not require login or payment. The app is an independent Japanese-learning product and is not affiliated with the official JLPT organizations. All practice questions are original.

Native iOS features that distinguish the app from the website include:
1. Japanese listening playback is intercepted from study questions and rendered with AVSpeechSynthesizer using a native ja-JP voice.
2. Users can configure a recurring daily study reminder using local UNUserNotificationCenter notifications; reminder settings stay on device.
3. Native Share and Settings interfaces are provided.
4. Google UMP consent/privacy options are integrated before AdMob ad requests where required.
5. Study answers and adaptive history are primarily persisted locally on the device.

No reviewer account is required. To test: choose N5–N1, select a study tab, answer questions, and use any listening play button. Open the gear icon to test the daily reminder and privacy settings.

## Data disclosure working notes
The app itself does not collect account identity or upload study answers to a developer-owned user database. However, Google AdMob/UMP and hosting/CDN services may process device/app identifiers, approximate location, advertising interactions, diagnostics, IP address and standard request metadata. Store privacy questionnaires must be completed consistently with Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosures and actual configuration at submission time.
